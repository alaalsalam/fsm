# Equipment 360 model

Installed Equipment is the durable service-life record. Identity, QR, item/serial, customer/site/location, sales origin, warranty, last service and fire-safety profile live on it. Jobs, visits, inspections, failures, materials, workshop, reports and certificates remain linked records and appear through history/projection APIs.

Project is intentionally not equipment ownership. Current and future maintenance are derived from active Plan/Occurrence links.
