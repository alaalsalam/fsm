# After-sales flow

`Customer → Sales Order optional → submitted Invoice/Delivery → Draft Service Contract → Delivered Equipment → Confirm Installation → Review/Activate Contract → Draft Maintenance Plans → Review/Activate Plans → Recurring Occurrences → Job → Visit → Report`.

## Automation and review boundaries

- A submitted non-return Sales Invoice or Delivery Note containing an FSM-serviceable Item prepares one Draft Service Contract. The shared Sales Order, Sales Invoice, or Delivery Note key prevents duplicate contracts across retries and normal ERPNext document conversion.
- A submitted Delivery Note remains the only equipment-provisioning trigger. Draft and return Delivery Notes are excluded.
- Installation confirmation adds the exact delivered equipment and Customer Site to the linked Draft Contract. The source-side `fsm_service_contract` field keeps ERPNext and FSM navigation bidirectional.
- The user reviews dates, coverage, entitlements, SLA, equipment scope, and maintenance cadence before activating the contract. A sales-generated Contract cannot activate until delivered equipment has a confirmed site.
- Activating a sales-generated Contract creates one idempotent Draft Maintenance Plan per covered Customer Site. It does not activate the plans.
- Activating the reviewed plans is the explicit schedule approval boundary; Maintenance Occurrences are then generated by the canonical occurrence engine.

`After-Sales Setup` is a read-only projection rooted in one submitted Delivery Note. It only shows the contract linked to that Delivery Note, plans linked to that contract, and occurrences linked to those plans. Its Delivery Note picker is server-filtered to submitted, non-return documents within the manager's company scope.
